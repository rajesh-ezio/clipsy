# The QC pass

Run on every clip before the picks table goes to the user, and again on the rendered
file before hand-over. Answer each question Yes / No / Fix from the clip's final
transcript (after tightening and caption fixes) and frames from the clip's time range.
A clip ships only with all Yes. Put every No or Fix in the table's Notes column with
the fix.

Questions 1-2 are the user's own; the rest come from clips the user sent back.

## Content
1. **Self-contained?** A stranger who never saw the session understands the whole clip.
2. **Opens on its topic?** The first two sentences say what the clip is about, using the
   topic word (the title only shows in the sheet - the viewer sees only the video).
3. **One takeaway?** You can write the post's one-line lesson from the transcript alone:
   a framework, a number, a mistake or a repeatable tactic - not a list.
4. **Ends on a finished point?** No mid-thought ending, no "let me take questions", no
   "let's move on".
5. **Nothing points outside the clip?** No "as I told you", "we spoke about that",
   "earlier", "next week", "this week", "your question", a participant's name or their
   company.
6. **Every who is explained?** Each "we", company, person, book or tool is either explained
   by him or given a pop-up.

## Edit
7. **Only his voice?** No participant audio, no "Mhmm", no audio from a video played on
   screen.
8. **No garbled or repeated lines?** Read it end to end: no false starts, restatements or
   sentences that don't parse.
9. **No stretch drags?** No 30+ seconds without a new point. 5 minutes max unless the user
   overrides.

## Screen
10. **The screen matches the words?** The slide on screen is about what he's saying. It is
    never an unrelated slide, a bare title slide or the Zoom gallery for long stretches,
    and the clip never joins a slide mid-build. A mismatched title can be fixed with
    `slide_cover` (added text over the slide - SKILL.md step 6); a Zoom-gallery-only clip is
    dropped.
11. **Captions correct?** Names, jargon and numbers are right on screen, and every caption
    fix landed (none split across a line break).
12. **Pop-ups right?** Each one is needed, factually correct, neutral (no "the presenter's former
    company"), shown at the moment of need, not already on the slide, clear of slide
    content, and never two at once.

## Post-safety
13. **Safe to post?** No unverified claim about a named company or person (who paid whom,
    who copied whom), no confidential numbers, no insults or profanity, and no historical
    fact he gets wrong.
14. **Timeless?** Nothing that dates it: "this year", "right now in 2023", "next week we'll
    cover...".
15. **Stops the scroll?** The first five seconds give a reason to keep watching: a claim, a
    number, a question or a surprise.
